# Milestone 5

The effort board is the north star: **delivery score 100 with 0 fixes**.

The leftover ~64 / 100 “other” work was not a new product surface. It was the same quality loop failing on small two-company packs — a Harbor name split, a 10-slide market deck rebuilt for a short briefing, an empty slide, and `$24 million` missing from the deck.

## What changed

- Related company names merge (`Harbor & Pine` / `Harbor & Pine Outdoor Co.`).
- Two-company short briefings stay a comparison deck. Only 10-slide / large landscape work uses the market template.
- Corrected decks must include company names, money figures, sources, and uncertainty. Empty slides are filled before they are written.
- `$24M` and `$24 million` count as the same figure.
- Harbor-in-the-company-list still counts as Harbor on the effort board.

Review, effort labels, desk opens, task identity, real denies, and the audit stay as they were.

## What this is not

Not a new review screen. Not enterprise. The person still accepts, requests one fix, or rejects.
