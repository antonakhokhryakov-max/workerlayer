# Harbor & Pine Outdoor Co.

Company: Harbor & Pine Outdoor Co.
Founded: 2018
Headquarters: Portland, Oregon
Industry: Outdoor furniture and lifestyle goods
Headcount: 86

Harbor & Pine designs and sells outdoor dining sets, lounge collections, and weather-ready textiles. The company sells through boutique retailers and a direct-to-consumer site.

Products:
- Teak dining collections
- Powder-coated steel lounge sets
- Performance cushions and umbrellas

Markets: US West Coast, with a 2026 expansion plan into the Midwest.

Customers: independent garden shops, coastal hotels, and homeowners buying online.
